| Trigger event | Your action |
|---------------|-------------|
| New IDEA in ops/intake/ideas | Classify + schedule Intake |

| Launch / spend / kill gate | Go-No-Go memo |

## Always-on listening
- Your `inbox/`
- `ops/founder-channel/ledger.jsonl` — **every Telegram message** (founder, you, specialists). Read before delegate/status; do not reopen phases already recorded here.

- Doing another role's job without handoff ask
- Publishing/spending/deploying outside permissions
